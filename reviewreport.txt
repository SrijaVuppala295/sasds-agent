PROJECT OVERVIEW
----------------
The SASDS Agent is an AI-powered system designed to provide comprehensive academic support 
and development assistance to students. The system aims to help students with:
- Academic planning and course selection
- Study guidance and resource recommendations
- Progress tracking and performance analysis
- Personalized learning path development
